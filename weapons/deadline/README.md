# DEA(c)DLINE

[Download 1.3.0 for HellForge](https://github.com/RojoMojo666/HellForge-Mods/raw/refs/heads/main/downloads/DEADLINE-Railgun-Stock-Blast-Per-Player-v1.3.0-build25480438.zip)

Overcharge protection for the **RS-422 Railgun** and **PLAS-45 Epoch**. Charge the weapon as usual and release the trigger when you want to fire.

## Railgun blast

The Railgun gets a **2 m impact blast** at every charge level. It uses the stock Railgun round with its regular direct damage and penetration. The current blast uses the game's existing Scorcher explosion.

The Epoch gets overcharge protection while keeping its regular shots.

**HellForge build 1.3.0.** The previous full-charge-only version and damage diagnostics are separate builds.
