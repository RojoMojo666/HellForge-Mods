# P-92L Warrant 12/12

[Download 0.2.1 for HellForge](https://github.com/RojoMojo666/HellForge-Mods/raw/refs/heads/main/downloads/P-92-Warrant-12x2-Mini-WASP-Round-Test-v0.2.1-build25480438.zip)

This test build adds a Warrant-style secondary with two selectable feeds. Each feed holds 12 rounds. Hold **Reload** to open weapon options and switch using the **Magazine** selector.

| Feed | Capacity | Standard / durable damage | Penetration | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Standard | 12 | 140 / 60 | 4 / 4 / 3 / 1 | Ballistic round |
| Mini-WASP | 12 | 140 / 110 | 4 / 4 / 4 / 3 | WASP burst effects, a 300-damage impact blast with a 2.5 m outer radius, and solo-only steering |

## Requirements

Use HellForge 0.5.0 beta (SDK 20, game build `25480438`) with synced ownership of the P-92 Warrant and P-69 Veto. HellForge compiles the source included in the ZIP. The build has no Chef or Bingus requirement.

## Try it in a solo session

Import the ZIP through **Mods → Add mods**, disable any earlier Warrant test build, and enable **Warrant 12/12 Mini-WASP Round Test**. Start a fresh solo session and equip **P-92L Warrant 12/12** in your secondary slot. Fire three Standard rounds and one Mini-WASP round from full feeds, then switch back and forth and check for counts of 9 and 11.

HellForge lists this as a Gameplay mod. The selector, feed counts, blast, and steering still need an in-game test.
